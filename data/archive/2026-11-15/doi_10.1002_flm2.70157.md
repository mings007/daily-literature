## 摘要
<jats:title>Abstract</jats:title>
                  <jats:p>Near‐infrared‐IIb (NIR‐IIb) fluorescent imaging with low tissue autofluorescence and high‐resolution visualization of microscopic structures plays a pivotal role in modern precision medicine. However, most NIR‐IIb fluorescent molecules are excited by NIR‐I light and limited to aggregation‐induced quenching, making the development of bright NIR‐IIb‐emitting fluorophores challenging. To address the above challenges, phenyl and tert‐butylphenyl groups were programmatically incorporated into the Et‐1080 molecule to afford Ph‐cy with an expanded π‐conjugated system and t‐Bu‐cy with enhanced π‐conjugation as well as amplified steric hindrance, respectively. The as‐prepared dyes exhibit maximum absorption wavelengths above 1050 nm along with NIR‐IIb emission, whose spectral tail extends to 1700 nm. Notably, the photophysical properties, theoretical calculations, and aggregation‐state analyses collectively verify that the extended conjugation and steric‐hindrance effects can effectively improve the NIR‐IIb emission performance of the cyanine skeleton in its nano micelle. Finally, t‐Bu‐cy nanoparticles successfully fulfill NIR‐IIb vascular imaging in mice under 1064 nm laser excitation, achieving high spatial resolution and a signal‐to‐background ratio. This work provides new insights for constructing 1064 nm‐excited organic nanoparticles with suppressed aggregation‐caused quenching toward high‐contrast vascular NIR‐IIb fluorescence imaging.</jats:p>
