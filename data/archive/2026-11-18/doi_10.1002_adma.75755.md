## 摘要
<jats:title>ABSTRACT</jats:title>
                  <jats:p>Low‐dimensional organic near‐infrared (NIR) crystals hold tremendous potential for integrated photonics. However, simultaneously achieving two‐dimensional (2D) structural growth and low‐loss waveguiding within the NIR‐II region remains a formidable challenge due to one‐dimensional (1D) packing tendencies imposed by strong charge transfer (CT) interactions. In this study, we strategically choose the extended π‐conjugated structure donor dithieno[2,3‐d:2',3'‐d']benzo[1,2‐b:4,5‐b']dithiophene and the strong electron‐withdrawing acceptor tetracyanoquinodimethane, and report the first large‐scale preparation of 2D organic CT cocrystal microsheets with NIR‐II emission at 1050 nm via a gas‐phase self‐assembly. The strategically expanded conjugation framework not only further promotes the luminescence red‐shift but also introduces an interaction network with orthogonal directions, counterbalancing the intrinsic 1D stacking constraints brought by the strong CT interactions and guiding the crystals into a thermodynamically stable 2D morphology. The compact network plays a crucial role in endowing the material with exceptional structural and photothermal stability, as well as low‐loss optical waveguiding. Furthermore, attributed to the extremely low lattice‐mismatch rate, 1D‐2D branched heterostructures were constructed, which demonstrated efficient energy transfer (ET) and cross‐band optical signal conversion over 600 nm. This work breaks new ground in extending 2D organic cocrystals into the NIR‐II region and provides a promising material platform for low‐loss, and integrated NIR optoelectronic devices.</jats:p>
