## 摘要
<jats:p>
                    Near-infrared-to-visible triplet−triplet annihilation photon upconversion (TTA-UC) enables harvesting low-energy photons; however, extending nanocrystal-sensitized TTA-UC into the second near-infrared window (NIR-II) remains challenging because triplet sensitization is thermodynamically disfavored under sub-triplet excitation. Here, we demonstrate that lead-free AgBiS
                    <jats:sub>2</jats:sub>
                    nanocrystals facilitate phonon-assisted endothermic triplet sensitization, enabling efficient NIR-II-to-visible upconversion. Under the sub-bandgap single-photon excitation, AgBiS
                    <jats:sub>2</jats:sub>
                    nanocrystals exhibit anti-Stokes photoluminescence, suggesting strong exciton−phonon coupling mediated by surface states. When coupled with 5-tetracene carboxylic acid transmitters and rubrene annihilators, AgBiS
                    <jats:sub>2</jats:sub>
                    achieves 980 nm-to-visible upconversion with a record quantum yield of 6.22% (normalized to 100%) and an anti-Stokes shift of 0.95 eV. Time-resolved photoluminescence and ultrafast transient absorption spectroscopy support a surface-state-mediated uphill energy-transfer pathway coupled to low-frequency vibrational modes. Our work establishes phonon-assisted triplet sensitization as a strategy to overcome the NIR-II red wall for lead-free photon upconversion.
