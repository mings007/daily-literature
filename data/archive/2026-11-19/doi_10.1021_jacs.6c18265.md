## 摘要
<jats:title>Abstract</jats:title>
                  <jats:p>Optical measurements of neuronal membrane voltage in vivo are limited by light scattering at visible wavelengths. Fluorophores operating in the second near-infrared window (NIR-II) offer improved tissue penetration; however, the reported NIR-II dyes generally lack voltage sensitivity because their dynamic symmetric structures disfavor field-sensitive charge separation. Here, we report a chemically optimized family of asymmetric NIR-II voltage-sensitive dyes. The optimized probe shows 20% fluorescence changes per 100 mV and submillisecond response kinetics, together with membrane-associated fluorescence enhancement that suppresses the background. In combination with oblique NIR-II light-sheet microscopy, the probes enable through-skull recording of spontaneous and sensory-evoked neuronal spiking at single-cell resolution in the mouse cortex at depths of ∼200–300 μm in real time, resolving fast neural spike trains. These results establish an asymmetric NIR-II fluorophore design as a route for minimally invasive optical voltage imaging in vivo.</jats:p>
